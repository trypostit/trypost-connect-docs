YouTube connects through Google OAuth. How the browser flow works: [Connect accounts](/guides/connect-accounts).

## Content type

`youtube_short` — 1 video, required. 9:16. Max **3 minutes**.

No `meta` keys. Image-only posts are not supported.

## Limits

| Video | Duration |
|---|---|
| 1 GB (Connect upload cap) | 180 seconds |

MP4 or MOV.
