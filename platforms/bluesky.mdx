```
GET /social-accounts/connect/bluesky?redirect_url=…
```

`url` is a Connect-hosted page (`/connect/continue/bluesky?state=…`), not the Bluesky OAuth dialog.

## Content type

`bluesky_post` — caption optional. Up to **4** images **or** 1 video (never mixed). GIFs are kept as GIF. No `meta` keys.

## Limits

| Image | Video | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Connect image cap (10 MB); Bluesky re-encodes stills | 300 MB | 10 minutes |

JPEG / PNG / GIF / WebP. Video MP4 or MOV.
