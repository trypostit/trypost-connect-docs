X is never free — connecting it without a card is refused. See [Billing](/guides/billing). How the browser flow works: [Connect accounts](/guides/connect-accounts).

## Content type

`x_post` — text with up to **4** images or **1** video. Media is optional. GIFs are kept as GIF.

## Limits

| Image | Video | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 5 MB | 1 GB (Connect upload cap) | 20 minutes |
| JPEG / PNG / GIF / WebP | MP4 or MOV | |

Official: [Media best practices](https://docs.x.com/x-api/media/quickstart/best-practices).
