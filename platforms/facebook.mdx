Facebook OAuth, then Connect's Page picker when the login has more than one Page. How the browser flow works: [Connect accounts](/guides/connect-accounts).

## Content types

| Type | `content_type` | Media |
|---|---|---|
| Post | `facebook_post` | Up to 10 images or video. Caption optional. |
| Reel | `facebook_reel` | 1 video, required. 9:16. Max 90 seconds. |
| Story | `facebook_story` | 1 video, required. No separate caption. |

Optional `platforms[].meta.aspect_ratio`: `"1:1"`, `"4:5"`, `"16:9"`, `"original"`.

## Limits

| Type | Image | Video | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Post | 4 MB | 1 GB (Connect cap) | 240 minutes |
| Reel / Story | — | 1 GB | Reel 90s, story 60s |

JPEG / PNG. Video MP4 or MOV.
