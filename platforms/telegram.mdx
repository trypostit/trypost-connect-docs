Connect hosts a `/connect <code>` screen for the bot. How the browser flow works: [Connect accounts](/guides/connect-accounts).

## Content type

`telegram_post` — caption optional. Up to **10** images or video (albums). GIFs are kept as GIF. No `meta` keys.

## Limits

| Image | Video |
|---|---|
| 5 MB | 20 MB |

These are Telegram's URL-publish caps. JPEG / PNG / GIF / WebP. Video MP4 or MOV.
