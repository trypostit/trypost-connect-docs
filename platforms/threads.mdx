## Content type

`threads_post` — caption optional. Up to **10** images or video. Preferred aspect 4:5. No `meta` keys.

Deleting a published Threads post needs the `threads_delete` permission (100 deletes/day/account).

## Limits

| Image | Video | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 8 MB | 1 GB | 5 minutes |

JPEG / PNG / WebP. Video MP4 or MOV.
