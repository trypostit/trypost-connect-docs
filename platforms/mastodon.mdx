---
title: Mastodon
description: Posts on any instance. Connect asks for the instance URL, then runs OAuth there.
icon: "mastodon"
---

```
GET /social-accounts/connect/mastodon?redirect_url=…
```

`url` is a Connect-hosted instance form, then the instance's OAuth.

## Content type

`mastodon_post` — caption optional. Up to **4** images or video. GIFs are kept as GIF. No `meta` keys.

## Limits

| Image | Video |
|---|---|
| 10 MB | 40 MB |

JPEG / PNG / GIF / WebP. Video MP4 or MOV.
