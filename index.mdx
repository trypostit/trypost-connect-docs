TryPost Connect is **B2B publishing infrastructure**. You build the scheduler, agent, or product. We run OAuth, token refresh, media hosting, native publish, and webhooks across 12 networks.

It is **not** a calendar, composer, or agency suite. There is no asset library and no self-host install. Connect is a closed-source Cloud product at [https://connect.trypost.it](https://connect.trypost.it).

## Surfaces

Same account, three callers:

| Surface | Who uses it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **REST API** | Your backend | Full contract: connect, media, posts, sets, webhooks, keys, logs |
| **MCP** | Agents | Same account. Operate connected accounts, posts, and webhooks. No connect tool — OAuth needs a browser. |
| **Web** | Your team | Ops dashboard: accounts, keys, billing, playground. Not your end-user UI. |

## What you host vs what we host

You keep the editor, queue, and brand. Connect hosts:

- OAuth and credential screens (including Bluesky, Mastodon, and Telegram)
- Token storage and refresh
- Direct-to-storage media upload
- Native publish to each network
- Outbound webhooks

Media is pass-through: ask for an upload slot, put the file, attach that public URL on the post. External URLs are rejected. There is no library to browse later. See [Media](/guides/media).

## Billing

Two non-X accounts are free. X always counts as 1. Extra non-X accounts are $3 each / month.

```
quantity = xCount + max(0, nonXCount - 2)
```

Connecting beyond the free allowance (or connecting X) without a card is refused. See [Billing](/guides/billing).
